# Kampus.VC — End-to-End Demo Script & Walkthrough

This script provides a step-by-step demonstration walkthrough showcasing Kampus.VC marketplace capabilities.

---

## Walkthrough Sequence

### Step 1: Public Landing & Discovery
1. Navigate to `/` (Landing Page).
2. Explore hero section, value proposition, featured creators, and campaign brief showcases.
3. Click `/explore` or `/creators` to test real-time search, skill tags, and filters.

### Step 2: Public Profiles & Creative DNA Passport
1. Select a creator profile (e.g. `/creator/<id>`).
2. Review portfolio gallery items, video reels, verified proof of creativity badges, availability indicators, and Creative DNA score visualization.

### Step 3: Brand Brief Creation & AI Assistance
1. Sign in as Brand (`brand@demo.com` / `Password123!`).
2. Navigate to Brand Dashboard `/brand/dashboard`.
3. Click **"Create Campaign Brief"**.
4. Test **"AI Magic Enhance"** to auto-generate creative requirements, deliverable milestones, and budget bounds.
5. Save and Publish brief.

### Step 4: AI Matching & Creator Proposals
1. Sign in as Creator (`creator@demo.com` / `Password123!`).
2. Discover published briefs under `/briefs`.
3. Inspect explainable creator match score percentage and breakdown.
4. Submit a tailored proposal with proposed milestones and portfolio attachments.

### Step 5: Proposal Acceptance & Project Milestone Workflow
1. Switch to Brand Dashboard.
2. Review submitted proposal, examine creator fit evidence, and click **"Accept Proposal"**.
3. Automated project & milestone creation triggers under `/projects/<id>`.

### Step 6: Messaging, Work Submission & Payment Sandbox
1. Open project workspace `/projects/<id>`.
2. Exchange real-time Socket.IO messages in chat `/messages`.
3. Creator submits milestone deliverable.
4. Brand initiates Razorpay sandbox payment checkout.
5. Verify milestone approval and financial ledger entries.
